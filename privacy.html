Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Applies to all apps published by Krish Bhingradiya on Google Play

This policy explains how the mobile apps published by Krish Bhingradiya ("we", "us") handle your information. It applies to every app listed under our developer name on Google Play. Some apps have extra details, given under Our apps below; where an app's section says something more specific, that section applies.

Our approach

  • Our apps work without an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use them.
  • What you enter in an app is stored on your device. We do not run servers that collect or store it.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Some features rely on trusted third-party services (listed below), which receive only what they need.

Information stored on your device

Content you create in our apps (for example records, lists, settings and preferences) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data, use the app's reset option where there is one, or uninstall the app. Backup or export files are created only when you ask, and saved where you choose.

Third-party services

Our apps may use the services below. Each app's section lists which ones it uses. These services process data under their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Free versions of our apps may show ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show personalised ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in the app's settings (for example "Privacy choices"). Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Permissions

Our apps ask only for the permissions a feature needs, such as internet access or notifications. Android shows you each runtime permission before it is granted, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings. Each app's section lists the permissions it uses.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to manage the purchase and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • Data held by Google or other services: as described in their privacy policies.

Security

Our apps use encrypted connections (HTTPS) for network requests. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit what leaves your device to what each feature needs.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our apps, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent.

  • Most data lives only on your device, so you can delete it yourself by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
  • You can withdraw ad consent in the app's settings and manage your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • You can decline AI features; nothing is sent to an AI service without your agreement.
  • For anything else, including data held by the services above that is linked to your device, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

International transfers

The third-party services above may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

Our apps

Gold AI: Gold Price Tracker

Tracks gold and silver prices and the gold and silver you own.

  • On your device only: holdings, purchase prices, notes, dealer names, portfolios, price alerts and settings. Delete them with Settings > Reset app and delete all data, or by uninstalling.
  • Prices and news: requests to gold-api.com (gold, silver, platinum, palladium and copper), Swissquote, xaus.com, Frankfurter and Coinbase; as backups, Metals.dev, MetalpriceAPI and Open Exchange Rates; for local prices, Goodreturns (India), Truncgil (Turkey), the Gold Traders Association (Thailand), PNJ (Vietnam) and Dubai City of Gold (UAE); and the RSS feeds of The Economic Times, Business Standard, BusinessLine, Mint, FXStreet, Investing.com, CNBC, Khaleej Times and The National. These are ordinary web requests: no portfolio data is included.
  • Firebase: Crashlytics, App Check (which uses Google Play Integrity to confirm requests come from the genuine app) and Remote Config.
  • AI Insights (only after you agree): portfolio totals and shares (such as total value, gain or loss, gold vs silver share and how they moved), market figures, price ranges worked out on your device, today's news headlines and your country are sent to Google Gemini. Holding names, notes and dealer names are never sent. Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Up to 3 AI refreshes a day; the daily count is kept on your device.
  • "If you sell today", break-even and tax figures, and price alerts (including profit-target and dip alerts) are worked out on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Subscriptions: none at the moment. If Gold AI Premium is offered later, purchases go through Google Play and RevenueCat, only if you subscribe.
  • Ads: Google AdMob (banner, native, app open and interstitial ads). A future Premium subscription may remove them.
  • Notifications: price alerts are checked on your device and shown as local notifications; there is no push server.
  • Permissions: internet; notifications (asked when you create your first alert); advertising ID (for AdMob). It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • Not financial advice: prices are indicative, and the app is for information and education only.
